Optimizing Fleets, Routes, & Warehouses

In logistics, margin success depends entirely on transit latency, load optimization, and real-time inventory accuracy. PeopleNTech designs enterprise fleet tracking networks, custom WMS portals, and automated dispatch workflows that keep logistics supply chains running without delays.

We leverage geospatial indices (PostGIS) and real-time socket connections to map driver coordinates, automate routing matrix updates, and sync multi-location inventories instantly with billing and ecommerce portals.

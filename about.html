// Our Story

How It All Began

PeopleNTech is a USA-based IT training and software solution provider company. Although it debuted as a staffing agency in the USA in 2005, it is now successfully operating as a premier IT training, job placement, and software solution provider with operations across Bangladesh, Canada, India, and the United States.

Founded by Engr. Abubokor Hanip, the company was established to address the rapid changes in technology requirements. We discovered that traditional classroom theoretical structures fell short, and upskilling potential professionals through intensive project-oriented workspaces was the most effective way to fill modern tech openings.
